Privacy policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Turf Tally is an Android app that helps the organizer of a weekly football game record attendance, split the cost of the pitch, and track payments. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

Who uses the app

The app is used by one organizer, who signs in with their own Google account. Players do not use the app and do not sign in.

What data the app handles

  • Game records: game dates, times, pitch names and costs.
  • Player records: player names and phone numbers that the organizer enters or picks from their own phone contacts, plus who played in each game, what each player owes and what they have paid.
  • The organizer's Google account email address, used only to show which account is connected and to disconnect it later.

Google account access

The app asks Google for two permissions:

  • drive.file: access only to the files the app itself creates or that the organizer opens with it. The app creates one spreadsheet and works only with that file. It cannot see or change any other file in the organizer's Google Drive.
  • email: the organizer's email address, for the reason given above.

Turf Tally's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored

  • In the organizer's own Google spreadsheet, in their Google Drive. The organizer controls it and can view, edit, share or delete it at any time.
  • In a copy on the organizer's phone, kept in the app's private storage so the app works without a signal. This copy is excluded from phone backups.

Turf Tally has no server of its own. The app talks only to Google's services, over encrypted connections.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party software development kits.
  • It does not read the phone's whole contact list. When the organizer adds a player from contacts, Android lets the app read only the one contact the organizer picks.
  • It does not send messages by itself. Reminders open WhatsApp or SMS with a message ready, and the organizer chooses whether to send it.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Disconnect in the app's settings revokes the app's access to the Google account and deletes the copy on the phone.
  • Access can also be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the copy on the phone.
  • The spreadsheet stays in the organizer's Google Drive until they delete it.

Children

The app is intended for adult organizers and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
