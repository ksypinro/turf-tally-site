Turf Tally

Turf Tally is an Android app for the organizer of a weekly football game. It records who played, splits the turf cost between them, and tracks who has paid.

Your data stays yours. Everything is kept in one Google spreadsheet that the app creates in the organizer's own Google Drive. There is no Turf Tally server, no account to create, and no analytics.

Turf Tally is used by its organizer and is not offered on public app stores.
